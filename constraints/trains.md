# Train loading

## max_inserters_per_wagon = 12

Maximum number of inserters that can access a single cargo wagon,
both sides combined.

**Open question**: a Steam forum thread claims up to 24 is possible
using long-handed inserters in a double row; not confirmed by the
wiki page itself. Treat 12 as the verified value, 24 as unverified
until checked against a primary source.

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Cargo_wagon
Verified: 2026-08-06
