# mall

A dedicated production area that automates crafting of items the
player repeatedly needs by hand or in bulk for construction — belts,
inserters, power poles, pipes, rails, modules, ammo, robots/roboports,
and similar — rather than crafting them manually or one-off. Also
called a "hub." Items are pulled from storage chests (belt-fed or
bot-fed) as needed, instead of hand-crafting each one from raw/
intermediate materials at the point of use.

Distinct from the main production chain that makes a base's actual
science-pack/rocket output: a mall exists to serve the *player* and
ongoing construction, not to feed the bus onward to another consumer.

Community-coined, not an official Wube term — usage confirmed across
both a dedicated forum build thread and general community discussion:
> "i want an evolve-able mall from mid game into the late game for
> mega base (raw mats from a main bus with conveyor belt..."

Referenced as standard troubleshooting advice for new players stuck
scaling their factory (e.g. r/factorio thread on a player running out
of iron/steel after automating blue science): *"Get a Mall - assemblers
building miners, smelters, and other building-items that you will
need a lot of."*

Also listed on the official wiki's own community-maintained Glossary
page, corroborating the term independent of the forum/Reddit sources
below.
