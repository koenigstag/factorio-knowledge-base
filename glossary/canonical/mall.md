# mall

A location in a factory that produces many different item types the
player needs, each in relatively small quantities — assemblers
crafting belts, inserters, turrets, and other buildings/intermediates
for construction, instead of hand-crafting them or building a
dedicated production line per item. Also known as hub or store.
