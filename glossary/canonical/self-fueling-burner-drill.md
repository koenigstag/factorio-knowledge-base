# self-fueling burner drill (pair)

Two `burner-mining-drill`s placed facing each other on a coal deposit
refuel each other automatically — each drill's own mined coal output
becomes the other drill's fuel input, no inserter or belt needed.
Together they can bank up to 100 coal (50 fuel-stack each). Runs
indefinitely off the coal patch itself, entirely independent of the
electric grid.

Direct wiki quote:
> "Two burner mining drills can refuel each other when placed next to
> each other on a coal deposit as long as they're facing each other."
