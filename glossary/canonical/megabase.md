# megabase

A base whose scale has grown past a single main-bus/city-block site
into a network of specialized, often remote, sites — the umbrella this
project's own `layouts/scalable_main_base.md` (the main base, where the
mall and science row live), `layouts/scalable_chem_base.md`, and the
planned `layouts/solar_base.md`/`layouts/nuclear_base.md` compose
under, once more than one of them exists at once.

Official Wube glossary, not just community shorthand:
> "A community term referring to a significantly large base that has
> been built over a significant time/effort." (wiki.factorio.com/Glossary)

The wiki's own definition is deliberately loose (no fixed number); the
community fills in a practical benchmark on top of it: **1,000 SPM**
(science packs per minute, each pack type — military science often
excluded) is the commonly cited threshold, with **10,000+ SPM**
sometimes distinguished as a "gigabase." Community sources also note a
qualitative marker independent of raw output: a base graduating from
one main-bus site into **outposts** — the wiki's own related term,
*"a small base created far away from a main base, usually to serve a
single purpose like mining or production of a specific resource"*
(wiki.factorio.com/Glossary) — is itself a sign of "megabase" territory,
which is exactly the shape `layouts/scalable_chem_base.md` (and the
planned solar/nuclear sites) take relative to
`layouts/scalable_main_base.md`: each is a single-purpose remote site,
not a scaled-up copy of the main bus.

Sources: https://wiki.factorio.com/Glossary (official Wube glossary —
"Megabase," "Outpost," "SPM" entries); community forum/blog discussion
for the 1,000/10,000 SPM benchmarks, lower confidence than the wiki
entries above (no single authoritative source for the exact number,
widely repeated but not official).
