# daisy chaining

A technique for supplying consumables (fuel, ammo, science packs) to
entities that let inserters both insert and remove items from the same
slot — chaining machines together with an inserter pulling from one
and feeding the next, instead of running a belt to each one
individually. Also called "stealing" (e.g. "ammo stealing", "coal
stealing"), since each entity in the chain takes what it needs and
passes the rest on down the line.

Two direct applications: turret ammo supply (a line of turrets sharing
one ammo feed point) and science lab supply (feeding one lab, letting
inserters redistribute packs to the next) — same mechanic, different
consumable.
