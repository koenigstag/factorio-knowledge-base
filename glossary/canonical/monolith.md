# monolith (monolithic base)

A base-organization approach: one site takes in raw materials (ore,
etc.) and does all processing through to science packs, rather than
splitting production across separate connected units. The opposite
end of the spectrum from [city-block](city-block.md),
[micro-factory](micro-factory.md), and [train-base](train-base.md),
which all split production into repeatable, physically distinct
pieces instead of one continuous site.

Community-coined, not an official Wube term. Usage is consistent
across multiple forum/Steam discussions (e.g. a "10K Monolithic Train
Base" build thread — the name alone confirms the term is in active
use), but no single thread states a clean formal definition the way
the main-bus wiki tutorial does. Practical tradeoff per general
base-design discussion: simpler to reason about early on (everything
in one place, no cross-module logistics to design), but harder to
scale past a point without hitting congestion, since there's no
repeatable unit to duplicate the way there is with the other three
approaches above.
