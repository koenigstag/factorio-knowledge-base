# port

The point where a `factory module` (see
`glossary/invented/factory-module.md`) connects to neighboring
infrastructure: resource, direction (in/out), side of the block,
offset from the corner, number of lanes, and its own inserter
tier/count — not inherited from the module as a whole (inserter
throughput needs can differ port to port even within one module). In
`factory-module.md`'s RAM-stick analogy, a port is the slot a module
plugs into.

Coined during initial architecture discussion; cross-reference updated
2026-08-07 when `module` was renamed to `factory module`.
