# chem base

An [outpost](../canonical/outpost.md) dedicated to oil processing and
everything chemically downstream of it (plastic, sulfuric acid,
batteries, lubricant, explosives, electric engine units) — this
project's `layouts/scalable_chem_base.md`. Exists as its own site
rather than a main-base block for two sourced reasons: crude-oil's
infinite-resource yield decay needs room to keep adding pumpjacks over
the field's lifetime, and most of what it produces is a fluid until
converted to a solid, which is cheaper to do next to the wellhead than
to ship raw fluids to the [main base](main-base.md).

Coined in `layouts/scalable_chem_base.md`; formalized here as part of
this project's base-type vocabulary alongside
[solar base](solar-base.md), [nuclear base](nuclear-base.md), and
[labs base](labs-base.md).
