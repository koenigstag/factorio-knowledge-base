# Early-game progression checklist (Nauvis start → first new planet)

Community strategy opinion, not a verified game mechanic — curated
here as a third-party reference rather than sourced fact (no
`Source:`/`Verified:` claim per `mechanics/`/`relations/`'s rule; see
"Provenance" below instead). The developers' own stated intent is that
the tech tree itself is the guide — this checklist is one community
member's breakdown of what that tech-tree progression looks like in
practice, organized around science-pack milestones.

## The checklist

1. **Basic mining with burners.**
2. **Electric mining** — fully automated iron, copper, stone, and coal.
   Red (Automation) science should be automated here.
3. **Stage 1 production** — fully automated inserters, assemblers,
   conveyors, ammunition. Build a healthy backlog of common components
   before scaling up. Green (Logistic) science should be automated
   here.
4. **Stage 2 production** — steel, walls, turrets, red ammo, grenades.
   Build a car and start scouting for more resources. Grey (Military)
   science should be automated here.
5. **Basic oil production** — pipeline to an outpost; add plastic,
   sulfur, and red circuits to the factory's output. Cyan (Chemical)
   science should be automated here. Build a tank (or several). Around
   here, starting resources typically run low — worth laying a couple
   of simple rail lines to funnel ores back to base (not full city
   blocks yet, just enough to keep growing).
6. **Advanced oil production** — batteries, electric engines, basic
   modules. Bolster power generation (possibly with solar). Learn
   simple circuit-based oil-cracking balancing. Purple (Production)
   science should be automated here.
7. **Start a roboport network** — not a full logistics replacement
   (power draw), just enough coverage to build anywhere freely. Yellow
   (Utility) science should be automated here.
8. **Late-base-game projects**, in whatever order: nuclear power, a
   city-block layout, launching the first rocket. Useful but not
   required to reach other planets.
9. **Start building spacecraft components** — asteroid crushers,
   grabbers, thrusters, platform foundation.
10. **Build the starting research satellite** — send up crushers,
    grabbers, and basic factory components; build the initial white
    (Space) science factory.
11. **Build the first spaceship** — a separate platform with thrusters,
    turrets, and more factory components, built up until it can move.
    Good logistic-network coverage of basic components plus roboport
    coverage makes remote factory maintenance easier here.
12. **Fly to the first planet.** Gleba, Vulcanus, and Fulgora can all
    be completed from a "naked start" (landing with nothing) — more
    fun, per the source, but easier with a robust Nauvis able to feed
    the new planet's starter base.

## Addendum (from a reply in the same thread)

- Automate solar power as early as practical.
- Get steel and green (logistic-tier) modules as early as basic oil
  and red circuits — both help significantly against biters.
